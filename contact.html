Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?
Visit our shop page, add products to your basket, and complete checkout. Your order is sent to us via WhatsApp and we'll follow up with payment instructions and confirmation.
How long does delivery take?
We dispatch within 1–2 working days of payment confirmation. UK delivery typically takes 2–3 working days via tracked courier. You'll receive your tracking number by email once dispatched.
How do I pay?
Payment instructions are provided after you place your order via WhatsApp. We accept bank transfer (BACS). We'll send you our payment details directly.
How is my order packaged?
All products are dispatched in discreet, unmarked packaging. Cold chain products are packed with gel ice packs and insulated liners to maintain the correct temperature during transit.
How do I verify my product is genuine?
Every product comes with a unique scratch-off verification code on the packaging. Visit our verify page and enter your code, or scan the QR code on your label.
Can I return or exchange a product?
Due to the nature of research compounds and cold chain requirements, we are unable to accept returns unless a product arrives damaged or is incorrectly supplied. Contact us immediately if there is an issue with your order.
Where can I find usage guides?
Comprehensive usage guides, dosing information, and reconstitution instructions are available on our Guides page for every product we stock.
