Official Product Documentation — Educational Purposes Only

Novigen Retatrutide®
Usage Guide

Please read all instructions carefully before use. Follow each step precisely to ensure safe and effective administration of your Novigen Retatrutide® pre-filled pen.

Retatrutide®Pre-filled injection pen  ·  Once weekly
⚠ Important — Read Before Use

This product is for use under the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional only. Keep out of reach of children. Do not use if the seal is broken or the solution appears cloudy or discoloured. Contact your prescriber immediately if you experience unexpected side effects.

🔬 Research Chemical Notice: Retatrutide is not yet approved for human use by any regulatory authority. This product is a research chemical intended for investigational purposes only. It has not completed clinical trials and its long-term safety profile in humans has not been fully established. Use is entirely at the individual's own risk.

Section 01 – Before You Begin
1

Check Your Pen

Before each use, inspect the pen carefully. Check the expiry date on the pen label. The solution should be clear and colourless or almost colourless. Do not use if you notice particles, cloudiness, or the seal is broken.

2

Allow the Pen to Reach Room Temperature

Remove from the refrigerator and allow to sit at room temperature for approximately 30 minutes before injecting. Cold solution may cause discomfort.

Do not warm using hot water or a microwave.
3

Wash Your Hands

Thoroughly wash and dry your hands before handling the pen or preparing the injection site to reduce the risk of contamination.

4

Gather Your Supplies

You will need your Novigen Retatrutide® pen, a new sterile needle, an alcohol swab, and a sharps disposal container. Always use a new needle for each injection.

Section 02 – Dosage
5

Starting Dose & Titration

Begin with a starting dose of 2mg once weekly. Your dose may be increased by 1mg at a time when clinically appropriate and as tolerated. Only increase under guidance of your prescriber.

Starting Dose2mgonce weekly
Titration Step+1mgwhen tolerated
Pen Capacity40mgper pen
~20 doses per pen at 2mg Never increase your dose without consulting your prescriber.
6

Injection Day

Administer on the same day each week, at any time of day, with or without food. If changing your weekly day, ensure at least 4 days have passed since your last injection.

7

If You Miss a Dose

If within 4 days of the missed dose, administer as soon as possible. If more than 4 days have passed, skip and resume on your next scheduled day. Do not double dose.

Section 03 – Administering the Injection
8

Choose Your Injection Site

Inject subcutaneously into the abdomen, front of the thigh, or upper arm. Rotate sites each week to avoid irritation.

Do not inject into a vein or muscle. Avoid bruised, red, tender, or broken skin.
9

Prepare the Injection Site

Clean the chosen area with an alcohol swab using a circular motion. Allow to air dry completely before injecting.

10

Attach the Needle & Check the Dose Counter

Attach a new sterile needle to the pen. Confirm the correct dose is set on the dose counter before injecting.

If you are unable to read the dose counter or have poor eyesight, do not use the pen without assistance from a person with good eyesight or your healthcare provider.

11

Inject the Dose

Gently pinch a fold of skin at the cleaned site. Insert the needle at 90° (or 45° if you have very little body fat). Press and hold the injection button until the dose counter reads zero. Keep the needle in skin for at least 6 seconds, then remove.

12

Remove & Dispose of the Needle Safely

Remove the needle and place directly into a sharps container. Never recap with two hands or dispose of in household waste. Replace the pen cap after every use.

Section 04 – Storage
13

Before Opening

Store unused pens in the refrigerator at 2°C to 8°C. Do not freeze. Keep away from the cooling element. Protect from light.

14

After First Use

Once in use, the pen may be kept at room temperature (below 30°C) for up to 6 weeks, or refrigerated for up to 16 weeks. Always replace the pen cap to protect from light.

Do not freeze a pen that has already been used.

Section 02 – Dosage
6

Dose Escalation Schedule

Novigen Tirzepatide® is started at a low dose and increased gradually to minimise gastrointestinal side effects. Your prescriber will guide your individual escalation schedule.

Section 03 – Administering the Injection
10

Choose Your Injection Site

Novigen Tirzepatide® is given as a subcutaneous (under the skin) injection. Recommended sites are the abdomen (at least 5cm from the navel), the front of the thigh, or the upper arm. Rotate sites each week to avoid skin irritation.

Do not inject into a vein or muscle. Avoid areas that are bruised, red, tender, swollen, or have broken skin.
11

Prepare the Injection Site

Clean the chosen area with an alcohol swab using a circular motion. Allow to air dry completely before injecting — do not blow or fan it dry.

12

Inject the Dose

Remove the inner needle cap. Gently pinch a fold of skin at the cleaned site. Insert the needle fully at a 90° angle. Press and hold the injection button until the dose counter reads zero. Keep the needle in place for at least 10 seconds to ensure full dose delivery, then remove.

You may hear a click when the injection is complete. Keep holding until the counter reads zero regardless.
13

Replace the Cap & Dispose Safely

Carefully remove the needle and place directly into a sharps disposal container. Never recap with two hands. Replace the pen cap after every use. Do not share your pen with anyone else.

Section 04 – Special Situations
14

Hypoglycaemia (Low Blood Sugar)

If using this product alongside a sulphonylurea or insulin, the risk of low blood sugar is increased. Warning signs include cold sweats, shakiness, pale skin, rapid heartbeat, confusion, or unusual hunger. Your prescriber may need to reduce your insulin or sulphonylurea dose.

Always carry a fast-acting glucose source (e.g. glucose tablets, fruit juice) if you are at risk of hypoglycaemia. Inform your prescriber if you experience these symptoms.

15

Driving & Operating Machinery

Hypoglycaemia can impair your ability to concentrate and react. If you experience symptoms of low blood sugar, do not drive or operate machinery until your blood sugar has returned to normal.

16

Pregnancy & Breastfeeding

Do not use this product if you are pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or breastfeeding. It is unknown whether Tirzepatide passes into breast milk. Contact your prescriber immediately if you become pregnant during treatment.

Recombinant Human Growth Hormone (rHGH) produced via E. coli expression. Please read all instructions carefully before use and only administer under the supervision of a qualified healthcare professional.

Section 01 – About This Product
1

What is Novigen rHGH 120IU?

Novigen rHGH is a recombinant human growth hormone produced using Escherichia coli (E. coli) bacterial expression technology. This produces a 191 amino acid sequence identical to endogenous human growth hormone. Each pen contains 120IU of somatropin.

1mg is approximately equal to 3IU. 1IU is approximately equal to 0.33mg. Growth hormone is most effective when administered in the evening or post-exercise to align with the body's natural pulsatile secretion.

Section 02 – Dosage Tiers

3

Tier 2 — Enhanced

Growth hormone is best administered once daily at Tier 1 doses. At Tier 2 and above, splitting into two doses (morning and evening, or post-workout and before bed) is widely recommended to better mimic the body's natural pulsatile release and reduce peak-related side effects.

Administering before bed is popular as it aligns with the body's largest natural GH pulse during deep sleep. Post-workout administration is also effective as exercise potentiates GH response.

Section 02 – Kit Contents

What's in Your Kit

Each kit contains 10 vials of lyophilised rHGH powder (24IU per vial), bacteriostatic water for injection, sterile syringes, and alcohol swabs. Total kit: 240IU.

Section 03 – Reconstitution
1

Prepare a Sterile Work Surface

Thoroughly clean your workspace with 70% isopropyl alcohol. Wash and dry your hands thoroughly. Use sterile gloves throughout the reconstitution process if available.

2

Check the Vial

Inspect each vial before use. The lyophilised powder should appear white or off-white. Check the expiry date. Do not use if the seal is broken, the powder appears discoloured, or the vial is cracked.

3

Wipe the Vial Tops

Clean the rubber stopper of both the HGH vial and the bacteriostatic water vial with a fresh alcohol swab. Allow to air dry for 30 seconds before proceeding.

4

Draw the Bacteriostatic Water

Using a sterile syringe, draw 1–2ml of bacteriostatic water. The volume used determines your final concentration — using 1ml gives a higher concentration per unit drawn, making smaller doses easier to measure accurately.

Most users use 1ml per vial. At 1ml: each 0.1ml (10 units on an insulin syringe) = 2.4IU.
5

Add Water to the HGH Vial

Insert the needle into the HGH vial at an angle and allow the bacteriostatic water to run slowly down the inside wall of the vial. Do not inject directly onto the powder. Remove the syringe once complete.

Never shake the vial — this denatures the protein and renders it ineffective.
6

Gently Swirl to Dissolve

Slowly and gently roll the vial between your palms in a circular motion until the powder is fully dissolved. The solution should be clear and colourless. If it remains cloudy or contains particles after gentle swirling, do not use.

7

Draw Your Dose

Using a fresh sterile insulin syringe, insert the needle into the reconstituted vial and draw the required volume for your dose. Refer to your concentration calculation to determine the correct volume.

Example: If you added 1ml bacteriostatic water to a 24IU vial, your concentration is 24IU/ml. A 2IU dose = 0.083ml ≈ 8 units on an insulin syringe.

Section 04 – Administration
8

Injection Site & Technique

Administer subcutaneously into the abdomen, front of thigh, or upper arm. Clean the site with an alcohol swab and allow to air dry. Pinch a fold of skin, insert at 90° (or 45° if lean), inject slowly, hold for 6 seconds, then remove. Rotate sites with each dose.

Do not inject into a vein or muscle. Avoid bruised, red, or broken skin.
9

Dispose Safely

Place used needles and syringes directly into a sharps disposal container. Never recap with two hands. Do not dispose of needles in household waste.

Section 05 – Storage
