Testing Methodology

HPLC — Purity Analysis
High-Performance Liquid Chromatography separates, identifies, and quantifies each component in the sample. Results confirm that the active peptide constitutes ≥98% of the total compound weight.
MS — Mass Spectrometry
Mass Spectrometry confirms the molecular weight and sequence identity of each peptide chain, ensuring the compound matches its stated chemical structure.
Independent Laboratory Testing
All testing is conducted by accredited third-party laboratories independent of Novigen. This ensures objectivity and compliance with research-grade quality standards.
Batch Testing
Every production batch is individually tested. COA documents are batch-specific and available on request for any product.
